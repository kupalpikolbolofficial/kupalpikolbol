INFO & POLICIES

Cancellation
Policy.

1. Customer Cancellation / Rescheduling

Customers may request a cancellation or rescheduling of their booking at least 12 hours before the scheduled playing time.

Requests made within the allowed period may be eligible for a refund or rescheduling, subject to confirmation by Kupal Pikolbol.

2. Less Than 12 Hours Before Booking

Cancellations or rescheduling requests made less than 12 hours before the scheduled playing time are generally not eligible for a refund or rescheduling.

3. No-Show

If a customer does not arrive for their scheduled booking and does not contact Kupal Pikolbol beforehand, the booking will be considered a no-show and will be non-refundable and non-reschedulable.

4. Weather / Unsafe Playing Conditions

If weather conditions or other circumstances make the court unsafe or unavailable, customers may request a refund or rescheduling.

Please contact Kupal Pikolbol through our official Facebook Page regarding weather-related cancellations or rescheduling.

5. Cancellation by Kupal Pikolbol

Kupal Pikolbol may cancel a booking when necessary due to unsafe court conditions, maintenance, facility issues, or other circumstances affecting court availability.

If Kupal Pikolbol cancels a paid booking, the customer will be contacted regarding the available refund or rescheduling arrangement.

6. How to Request a Cancellation or Rescheduling

For cancellation, refund, or rescheduling requests, please contact us through our official Facebook Page and provide:

  • Booking ID
  • Full name
  • Scheduled date and time
  • Reason for the request

INFO & POLICIES

Terms &
Conditions.

By making a booking with Kupal Pikolbol, you agree to the following terms and conditions.

1. Booking

  • All court bookings must be made through the official Kupal Pikolbol booking system or through an authorized Kupal Pikolbol representative.
  • A booking is considered submitted once the required booking information and GCash payment reference have been provided.
  • Players are responsible for providing accurate contact and booking information.
  • Each booking is valid only for the selected date, court, and playing time.

2. Payment

  • Court fees must be paid through the payment method provided by Kupal Pikolbol.
  • Customers must provide the correct GCash payment reference when submitting a booking.
  • Bookings may remain subject to payment verification before being considered confirmed.
  • Customers should keep their payment reference and Booking ID for their records.

3. Arrival & Use of Court

  • Players should arrive on time for their scheduled booking.
  • Court time is limited to the reserved period.
  • Players should vacate the court when their scheduled time ends so the next booking can begin.
  • Players are responsible for keeping the court and surrounding areas clean and orderly.

4. Court Rules & Player Conduct

Players are expected to follow reasonable facility and court rules, treat other players and staff with respect, use the court and equipment responsibly, and avoid behavior that may damage the facility or disturb other players.

Kupal Pikolbol reserves the right to address inappropriate or unsafe behavior.

5. Cancellation & Rescheduling

Cancellations and rescheduling are subject to the Kupal Pikolbol Cancellation Policy. Customers should review the Cancellation Policy before completing their booking.

6. No-Show

A customer who does not arrive for their scheduled booking and does not contact Kupal Pikolbol beforehand will be considered a no-show. No-show bookings are non-refundable and non-reschedulable.

7. Weather & Court Availability

Kupal Pikolbol may temporarily suspend or cancel play when weather, court conditions, maintenance, or other circumstances make playing unsafe or the court unavailable. Customers may contact Kupal Pikolbol through the official Facebook Page regarding available refund or rescheduling arrangements.

8. Facility & Personal Responsibility

Players are responsible for their personal belongings while inside the facility. Kupal Pikolbol is not responsible for loss, theft, or damage to personal belongings brought to the facility.

9. Safety

Players are responsible for playing within their own physical ability and following reasonable safety practices. Please use appropriate sports attire and footwear suitable for pickleball and the court surface.

10. Right to Refuse or Cancel a Booking

Kupal Pikolbol reserves the right to refuse, suspend, or cancel a booking when necessary due to unsafe conditions, facility or maintenance issues, violation of facility rules, inappropriate behavior, or other circumstances affecting safe and proper operation of the facility.

11. Contact

For questions, booking concerns, cancellations, rescheduling, or other inquiries, please contact Kupal Pikolbol through the official Facebook Page.

INFO & POLICIES

Privacy
Policy.

Kupal Pikolbol respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our booking service and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you make a booking, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address, if provided
  • Booking date
  • Selected court
  • Selected playing time
  • GCash payment reference number
  • Booking notes or other information you choose to provide

We also generate a Booking ID to help you check and manage your booking.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and manage your court booking
  • Verify your payment
  • Confirm or update your booking
  • Contact you regarding your booking when necessary
  • Respond to booking concerns, cancellations, or rescheduling requests
  • Maintain booking and payment records
  • Manage court availability and prevent double bookings
  • Improve the operation of Kupal Pikolbol's booking service

We do not require customers to create a member account to make a booking.

3. Payment Information

Kupal Pikolbol may collect your GCash payment reference number for payment verification. We do not ask customers to provide their GCash password, PIN, or other private account credentials through the booking system.

4. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information and limit access to authorized persons involved in managing Kupal Pikolbol bookings and operations. However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing of Information

Customer information is used primarily for Kupal Pikolbol's booking and operational purposes. We do not sell customer information.

Information may be accessed by authorized persons when necessary to process bookings, verify payments, manage the facility, respond to customer concerns, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

6. How Long We Keep Information

Booking and payment information may be retained as part of Kupal Pikolbol's operational records. Records may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary for booking management, payment verification, recordkeeping, and other legitimate business purposes.

7. Your Privacy Concerns

If you have questions or concerns about how your information is handled, please contact Kupal Pikolbol through our official Facebook Page.

Contact us on Facebook →

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Kupal Pikolbol may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any updated version will be made available through the Kupal Pikolbol website.
